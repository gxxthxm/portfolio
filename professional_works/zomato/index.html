Overview

During a short but impactful stint with India’s largest food delivery app, I collaborated with the design team to craft key elements for both marketing and UI, creating engaging visuals and user-friendly interfaces tailored to diverse consumer segments while maintaining brand consistency across all touchpoints. A significant focus was on App Store Optimization (ASO), ensuring greater visibility and conversions in a highly competitive market. I adapted designs to regional preferences, considering linguistic, cultural, and behavioral nuances to develop localized campaigns that resonated across various states. Despite being under NDA, the experience provided invaluable insights into designing for a large-scale, fast-paced platform, enhancing my adaptability, problem-solving, and innovation under pressure. I navigated complex workflows and tight deadlines while working closely with marketing and product teams to ensure that all design efforts aligned with data-driven strategies, maintaining consistency between in-app experiences and external campaigns.
