Overview

As a core creative contributor for IEEE LINK and IEEE SB RIT, I played a multidisciplinary role that blended branding, marketing design, UX, and team leadership. My work supported both internal and external communications, elevated event visibility, and ensured a consistent and professional visual identity across multiple IEEE student branches and conferences.

ieee-link.org

ieeesbrit.com

Visual & Marketing Design

  • Event Promotions: Led the design and production of compelling visuals for IEEE’s major events, including flyers, banners, digital ads, and motion graphics. These campaigns significantly boosted social engagement and increased on-site attendance.
  • Social Media Design: Designed branded social media assets with cohesive aesthetics that aligned with IEEE’s global identity while keeping content engaging for student communities.
  • Marketing Strategy Support: Collaborated with event coordinators to ensure visual materials aligned with promotional timelines and audience demographics. Leveraged post-event feedback to iterate on design approaches for future campaigns.

Branding & Identity

  • Brand Consistency: Co-developed and implemented comprehensive branding guidelines used across all IEEE SB RIT and IEEE LINK communications. This ensured consistency in logo use, typography, color schemes, and overall tone across print and digital media.
  • Visual Storytelling: Created design systems that unified the visual identity across workshops, conferences, and internal communications—enhancing brand recognition and professionalism across all touchpoints.

Presentation & Conference Assets

  • Designed engaging, high-impact presentation decks and print materials for workshops, speaker sessions, and student outreach programs.
  • Maintained a visual style that balanced academic credibility with creative energy, contributing to a more polished and engaging experience at IEEE-hosted events.

UX & Website Contributions

  • Website Redesign: Contributed to the UX and visual redesign of ieee-link.org and ieeesbrit.com, focusing on improving accessibility, navigability, and content hierarchy for students, professionals, and researchers alike.
  • UI Enhancements: Offered feedback on layout improvements, typography, and iconography to make the user interface more approachable and inclusive.
  • Responsive Design: Ensured websites maintained a responsive and accessible design across devices by aligning with modern web design standards.

Leadership & Mentorship

  • Team Management: Led a team of junior designers and volunteers, delegating tasks and ensuring smooth coordination for high-profile events like AKPESSC, AKIASSC, ZEUS, etc.
  • Skill Development: Mentored aspiring designers in areas such as UI/UX principles, Adobe tools, motion graphics, and brand design—many of whom have since pursued design roles professionally.

Impact

  • Events marketed under my creative leadership saw a significant increase in both registrations and online engagement.
  • Visual and UX improvements helped strengthen IEEE’s brand presence across Kerala’s student engineering community.
  • My branding frameworks and design templates continued to be used by IEEE SB teams long after my active involvement.
